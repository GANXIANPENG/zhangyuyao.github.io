关于我

本科毕业于贵州师范大学，现为四川音乐学院2025级手风琴与现代器乐系的硕士研究生。 深耕手风琴演奏领域，曾斩获2026年CMA世界手风琴锦标赛中国赛区成人流行组第一名及2026年美国演奏家手风琴组三等奖等专业大奖。 此外，积极投身各项音乐教育活动与舞台实践，展现了出色的音乐综合素养。

About me

Graduated with a bachelor's degree from Guizhou Normal University and currently enrolled as a master's student (Class of 2025) in the Accordion and Modern Instrumental Music Department at the Sichuan Conservatory of Music. Deeply dedicated to accordion performance, having won major professional awards including first place in the Adult Pop category of the China Division at the 2026 CMA World Accordion Championship, and third prize in the accordion category at the 2026 American Virtuoso Competition. Additionally, actively involved in various music education activities and stage performances, demonstrating outstanding comprehensive musical literacy.
